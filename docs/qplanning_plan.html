Q-Planning for the Pro 630

Self-improving the deployed suction pick policy without touching its weights: a small off-policy Q-critic over 0.5 s action chunks scores candidate chunks proposed by the frozen policy, learns from every twin and robot rollout including the failed ones, and is retrained between deployments.

Paper: Giridhar, Khandelwal, Collins, Georgiev, Garg, Beyond Imitation: Self-Improving Robot Policies via Off-Policy Q-Planning (q-planning.github.io, arXiv 2608.21204, code on GitHub). Our stack: rl/env_paper.py twin on mujoco_warp with the measured drive, rl/weights/resid3_fast_best (96.4 % in the twin, 3° bound), rl/real_policy_ctrl.py + spline controller on the Pi, two-camera success monitor.

What the paper does, in three sentences

A behaviour-cloned policy stays frozen; a separate Q-function over action chunks is trained off-policy with categorical (HL-Gauss) regression on sparse episode rewards. At run time N chunks are sampled from the policy, scored by Q, and the softmax-Q-weighted average chunk is executed. After each deployment all episodes, successes and failures, go into a replay buffer and only Q is fine-tuned; ten such iterations took LIBERO-10 from 90 to 99 % and two real bimanual tasks from 40→90 % and 25→80 %.

Why it fits us: the residual PPO already showed that the frozen base sets the pace and quality and a small correction on top is what improves (87.9→94.7→96.4 %). Q-Planning is the same idea moved from the weights to the action selection, and it consumes exactly the data we now produce every night: twin rollouts at 4096 worlds and robot runs with a camera success label.

Proposal distribution

The paper samples from a generative policy. Ours is a point estimate, so the N = 32 candidates are built around it:

  • 16 noisy rollouts. Unroll π for H = 5 steps in the twin's one-step model? No: too slow on the robot. Instead perturb π(ot) with chunk-correlated Gaussian noise (σ = 0.15 action units, shared across the chunk with a small per-step term), the same 0.005 observation noise the policy was trained with.
  • 12 structured variants. The same chunk scaled ×0.7, ×1.2, ×1.4 on the joint dims, and with the suction bit flipped. Our twin test showed that scaling blanketly in free space fails (96.7 → 88 % at ×1.3), but a Q-critic can choose the fast variant only in the states where it is safe. This is the honest route to the 1.2 s that the drive ceiling still allows in the approach.
  • 4 safety chunks. Hold, and slow-down to 50 %, so the planner can always pick "wait" near contact.
  • Later: once the CFM / diffusion policies of the IL v2 plan exist, they replace the noisy rollouts as the generative proposal, exactly as in the paper.

The self-improvement loop

Algorithm, adapted

buffer D ← twin rollouts of π (successes and failures), chunked with H = 5
repeat for k = 1..K:
    deploy: at each decision sample N chunks around π(o), score with Q_φ,
            execute the softmax-Q weighted chunk through the spline controller;
            the guarded press primitive still owns contact and the attach
    append every episode (obs, chunk, r_succ, r_time, done) to D
    retrain only Q_φ: HL-Gauss cross-entropy to the H-step bootstrapped target
            y = Σ_{i<H} γ^i r_{t+i} + γ^H Q_φ̄(o_{t+H}, chunk_π(o_{t+H})),  φ̄ = EMA(φ)
    gate on the paired deterministic eval before the next deployment

Risks and how the plan handles them

  • The critic can only choose what the policy can propose (the paper's own limitation). The structured variants widen the support where it matters (speed); a generative proposal comes with IL v2.
  • Q over-estimation on unseen chunks. HL-Gauss plus a small conservative term (penalise Q on proposal chunks far from π), and the 0.9·max success gate before any speed selection.
  • Label noise on the robot. The monitor was right on the two no-move runs but its fixed-camera localisation still needs the depth-first fix; until then the operator confirms each verdict, as tonight.
  • Contact. The planner acts in free space and during the carry; the guarded press, attach and place-down primitives stay in charge at contact, so a bad chunk cannot press into the table.
  • Twin-to-robot gap. The drive model now matches the deployed law to +25 % on peak speed; the tracker-error DR is the missing factor and is in M2.
