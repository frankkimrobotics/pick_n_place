What works on the robot

  • Deployed policy: DAgger round 10 (87.8 %) + bounded residual PPO (94.7 → 96.4 % with a 3° bound).
  • Spline-tracked 10 Hz reference on the Pi (K0 10, 45 ms lead): the 10 Hz sawtooth that looked jerky is gone in the 100 Hz logs.
  • Live colour/depth tracker, touch calibration of the object top, guarded press, scripted lift, guarded settle.
  • Two-camera before/after success monitor on the free D405s.

What was learned the hard way

  • Cross-drive transfer is dead: an 89 % ideal-drive policy scores 1 % on the measured drive. The twin must model the Pi law exactly.
  • Plain PPO from a DAgger init decays; a frozen base + small residual keeps the press behaviour.
  • The BC clone needs its training observation noise at test time (31 % → 78 %).
  • Object position and height errors, not the policy, caused most real failures; a wrong sign in the tracker bias put the cup on the rim.
  • The twin's suction righting spring was spinning light objects and breaking seals; fixing it lifted a teacher from 60 to 88 %.

Twin and drive model

The twin runs 4096 worlds of mujoco_warp with the deployed Pi law inside each 10 ms tick: u = vff·v_ref(t+lead) − K0(q − q_ref(t+lead)) − K1(q̇ − v_ref), clamped at 50 °/s, delayed 45 ms, acceleration-capped at 800 °/s², with a bounded 3° lead like the controller. The reference is the same uniform cubic B-spline the Pi welds.

Controller additions that came out of the series

  • Touch calibration of the object top before each run (camera tops were 1–6 cm low on cups).
  • Guarded press to a target contact force; flat hold chunks; scripted 8 cm lift after the attach.
  • Tracking freezes below 25 cm; blob-size sanity; follow the candidate nearest the current estimate.
  • Press descends at the estimated centre, 3 mm lateral per decision (the 50 °/s kick at contact).
  • Depth-first tracker: objects = anything 1 cm above the table plane, split into clusters, shadows excluded.

Calibration result

The D435 tracker's raw centre is +16 mm in y and +5 mm in x from the true lid centre, measured by a wrist-camera circle fit from a 12 cm hover and confirmed by eye on two objects. The earlier bias file had the opposite y sign, which is why the cup kept landing on the +y rim. Torque rim probing was tried and rejected: it pushes light cans.

Cameras: D435 (tracker, on a USB 2 hub), fixed D405 + wrist D405 (success monitor). A ZED 2i is ready in software (tracker + board calibration) but needs a USB 3 port.

Q-Planning on the twin

After Giridhar et al. 2026: the policy stays frozen; a small critic over 0.5 s action chunks (HL-Gauss, three heads: success, time, joint speed) scores N = 16 proposals built around the policy's action, a success gate keeps the top candidates, a soft speed gate drops the jerky ones, and the softmax-weighted chunk's first action is executed with a one-step EMA.

Self-improvement loop: flat in the twin

Ten iterations never beat iteration 0 (best 98.7 %, then decay). Two causes, both measured: the critic has a sharp optimum in TD steps (20k) that warm-started retraining walks off, and every failed twin episode is a physically impossible seal under DR, so re-ranking the same policy's chunks has nothing to learn from. The loop is worth running where failures are recoverable: on the robot, with the monitor as the label.

Details that mattered

  • Q_time collapsed onto Q_succ until the buffer had speed-scaled rollouts that mostly succeed at different times.
  • Jerk was the re-planning, not fast candidates and not the mixture; a hard velocity mask biased survivors slow.
  • The speed head needs its own trunk (shared: −1.3 pp).
  • Training the critic on corrupted-observation data made it worse; a clean critic still recovers 3–6 pp under tracker error.
  • Exported as one TensorRT graph (proposals, scoring, gates inside); loads like a policy; 0.24 ms per call.

Obstacles, larger cell, and the suction bug

env v3

  • Spawn x 0.20–0.56, y ±0.35; goals ≥ 10 cm from the walls (back −0.30, sides ±0.50).
  • 1–3 distractor bodies, a 0.10–0.25 m post on the carry corridor in 69 % of worlds; contact or >1 cm displacement = failure.
  • Observation 54-D: + nearest distractor's relative position, half-extents, count.
  • The old student drops 56 → 32 % here with 32 % obstacle hits.

Suction bug

The righting spring on the cup saturated after 1.1° of tilt and injected 30–230 rad/s of spin into light objects; the damper turned that into fake linear velocity and the seal broke by force saturation. Seal breaks 36 → 1 per 128 worlds after capping the torque and spin. Neutral on the deployed policy; the likely cause of the earlier diverse-object teacher ceiling at 50 %.
